In this post you will learn how to conjugate and declinate every noun and verb in the Arusian language. Since the language is mostly analytic/isolating (actually halfway), most of the declension and conjugation will be based on glueing other morphemes to the base root.

## Roots Ending in -ara or -uwa

|**Root**|duwa — *waste*|lara — *arrow*|luwa — *water*|
|-|-|-|-|
|**Transitive Agent Verb**|duwar — *to waste*|larar — *to point to*|luwar — *to wetten*|
|**Transitive Patient Verb**|duwase — *wasted by*|larase — *pointed at by*|luwase — *wetten by*|
|**Nominalised Agent Verb**|duwaa — *act of wasting*|laraa — *act of pointing*|luwaa — *act of wetting*|
|**Nominalised Patient Verb**|duwaai — *being wasted*|laraai — *being pointed at*|luwaaai — *being wet*|
|**Agent Noun**|su-duwaai — *one who wastes*|su-laraa — *one who points*|su-luwaa — *thing that wettens*|
|**Patient Noun**|su-duwaai — *thing that is wasted*|su-laraai — *one who is pointed*|su-luwaai — *thing that is wet*|
