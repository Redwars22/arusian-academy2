Complex NPs in Arusian, that is, NPs that don't have only a single noun/root inside, are usually nominalised CPs. Unlike in English, many **NP** structures with a noun, a determiner and an adjective are actually clauses in Aru. Beware that they often don't translate word for word between the two languages, so literal translations are extremely unlikely to fall apart.

Clauses in Aru usually have the form `X sa Y` (**SA** can undergo allomorphy to **-S** which is enclitic to the noun or **Ø** when the second element is a **VP**). `Su delras` is a **CP** meaning "he rules". If one wants to nominalise it and use it as if it were an **NP**, meaning "the one who rules" -- which is basically a "ruler", "governor" or "king" -- you replace the **SA** copula by **Ø**, which is the nominaliser. It yields  `su-delra`, which can be used as a noun.
